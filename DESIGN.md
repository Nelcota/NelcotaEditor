# Nelcota — design brief

Nelcota turns long videos into vertical short clips with captions. Since 2026-11-23 the app follows the
owner's second reference (a creators platform): a dark window by default, with greys that carry no hue
(the owner asked for nothing bluish) and blue as the one action colour (it was the brand green until
2026-11-23). The frame holds the
sidebar: the Editor Oculto logo, the bell (videos being cut) and the collapse button;
the sections (Início, Vídeos, Cortes, Painel, Conta, and the Admin folder for admins); the storage card
(used out of the quota, originals and clips, "Ver uso" and "Gerenciar"); the person with a menu. One
rounded panel holds the breadcrumb bar (theme toggle trailing) and the page; page titles live in the
breadcrumb and stay for screen readers only. Light stays available through the toggle.

- **Início** opens with a greeting from Nel beside the brand mascot (40px on phones, 56px from
  `sm`), a box to paste a YouTube link (it starts the video with the saved preferences, shown as
  pills; the plus opens the full form), and shortcuts.
- **Vídeos** is a table (status pill, source, clips, progress with the stage, date), columns by a
  container query so a narrow panel falls back to compact rows.
- **Painel** opens with the results card: totals, the clips ranked by score with bars, clips per day.
- **Cortes** are cards with the primary download button; a finished video's page opens with the summary
  bubble and its settings as tags.
- **Entrar / Criar conta** (direction B, 2026-11-23): the form wears the public site (`.theme-site`:
  lavender page, white fields, Geist, the bracketed eyebrow and the light/heavy title), so the step
  from the site keeps its look; from lg the dark app sits beside it (`.theme-app`): the link box and
  four finished clips, using the site's Pexels frames in `public/auth/`. Phones show the form only.

## Principles

- **One obvious next step.** Each screen has one primary action in ink (`primary-solid`). Everything else is
  secondary or ghost.
- **Always say what is happening.** Processing takes minutes, so every video shows its stage in
  plain words and a progress bar. Errors say what went wrong and what to do.
- **Content first.** Clip previews are large, at their real 9:16 shape.

## Tokens (`src/styles.css`)

| Token                                 | Use                                                    |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| `frame` (dark: near-black)            | Window frame behind the sidebar                        |
| `background` (dark: charcoal)         | The rounded panel                                      |
| `card` (a step above the panel)       | Filled boxes: search, table head, cards, sidebar cards |
| `secondary` / `muted` / `accent`      | Pills, tracks, hover fills                             |
| `primary` / `primary-solid` (blue)    | Primary buttons, current section, progress, focus      |
| `primary-ink`                         | Blue text, links and the sidebar icons                 |
| `muted-foreground`                    | Secondary text                                         |
| `success` (green) / `warning` (amber) | Finished videos, storage bars / warnings               |
| `destructive` (red)                   | Failures and delete                                    |

- Font: Inter (400 to 700). Big numbers and the greeting bold with tight tracking. No gradients
  (flat fills and the dot texture only).
- Radius: controls 10px (`rounded-xl`), panels and covers 12px (`rounded-md`, `rounded-2xl`), small
  chips 8px (`rounded-lg`). Buttons are never pills; round is kept for avatars, dots and the big play button.
- Scrollbar: a 4px strip.
- The caption highlight in rendered clips stays yellow (#FFE500): it is part of the clip, not the UI,
  and yellow reads best over any video frame.

## Type and icons

Raised one step on 2026-11-19: small grey text on the dark canvas was hard to read.

| Use                                                 | Size                  |
| --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Captions, counts, dates (`text-xs`)                 | 13px                  |
| Secondary lines: crew lines, metadata (`text-meta`) | 14px                  |
| Body, buttons, fields, lists (`text-sm`)            | 15px                  |
| Inputs (`text-base`)                                | 16px                  |
| Section titles (`text-lg`)                          | 18px                  |
| Page title                                          | 28px (`text-title`)   |
| Icons in buttons, fields, selects, menus            | 20px                  |
| Phone tab bar icons                                 | 24px (tabs 64px tall) |
| Small icons beside text                             | 16px                  |

- `text-xs` and `text-sm` are redefined in the `@theme` block of `src/styles.css`; never go below 13px.
- One icon set: Solar (linear). Icons help people find things: section titles, filters, sorts, metadata
  (source, date, clip count) and field labels carry one. Not where a mascot already stands (the crew,
  the Brilho score), and never an icon alone on a text that does not need finding.

## Mascots

Two 4x4 sprites in `public/mascots/` (sources in `design/assets/`), drawn by `Mascot` (index 0–31).
Each drawing has one meaning only:

- **Shape 15 (the green ghost) is Nel, the brand mascot** (home greeting, empty screens). Since
  2026-11-25 the logo is Editor Oculto's symbol (`BrandMark`): two lime clips with the play between them.
  It is reserved: no video or crew member uses it.
  It floats in the app frame and makes one gentle hop beside the home greeting. In the expanded
  sidebar and on Home, its eyes follow the pointer, taps or keyboard focus. It blinks at irregular
  intervals in the sidebar and once during the greeting on Home. On Home the eyes travel farther
  across the face, and Nel smiles at rest and up close or looks curious when following something
  farther away.
- **Shapes 0–14 are videos.** Each video gets one for life: `hash(video.id) % 15`
  (`src/modules/videos/model/video-mascot.ts`). Gallery cards and the video header.
- **Objects (16–31) are the crew**, one per stage (`src/modules/videos/model/stage-crew.ts`):

| Stage        | Crew      | Why                                                                |
| ------------ | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Queued       | Moon      | waiting, asleep                                                    |
| Downloading  | Lightning | speed                                                              |
| Transcribing | Octopus   | many arms writing                                                  |
| Selecting    | Star      | picks the stars                                                    |
| Rendering    | Fire      | heats up the clips                                                 |
| Done         | Clover    | it went well                                                       |
| Failed       | Shield    | something blocked it                                               |
| Cancelled    | Mushroom  | sits still, by choice (purple, so it never looks like the octopus) |

- The crew appears in the processing panel, the failed/cancelled panels, clip placeholders and
  empty/error states. It is decorative: the stage is always written next to it.
- The processing panel shows four larger mascots. Their layout uses two columns in narrow panels
  and four when the panel has room. The current member repeats its own gesture, and waiting or
  finished members shift gently out of sync. Upcoming members are grey and finished members
  have a check mark. The working mascot also repeats its gesture beside a video in the list.
- Account > Appearance controls mascot animations for this browser. They are on by default
  and pause in hidden tabs. Reduced motion keeps looping mascots in place with a gentle opacity
  change and keeps the brand's eyes still.
  Clover makes one small hop when the cuts summary sees a video finish. Shield shakes its head
  once when processing fails. Opening a video with a known result shows its mascot at rest.
- **The cast** (`src/modules/videos/model/crew-lines.ts`), friendly and discreet — one short line, always
  next to the facts, never instead of them: **Nel** (brand ghost, host of empty screens), **Luna**
  (queue), **Faísca** (download), **Tinta** (transcription), **Brilho** (best moments), **Brasa**
  (clips), **Sorte** (done), **Guarda** (failed), **Cogu** (cancelled).
- A name appears only beside its mascot or its role (the processing steps, the Painel crew cards,
  the Uso rows). Instructions, dialogs, errors and empty states say what happens in plain words
  ("Descobrimos o idioma sozinhos", "depois que a renderização terminar"), never "a Tinta" or
  "a turma" alone. The clip score reads "Nota", and its reason is always in Portuguese.

## Restraint

- Decoration must carry information. No glows, gradients, icon tiles or looping animation in forms
  and dialogs; the mascots appear where they mean something (a video, a stage at work).
- Left-align forms and dialogs; one primary button, right-aligned in dialogs.
- Navigation: the sidebar lists Vídeos (count of videos), Cortes (count of ready clips), Painel and,
  for admins, Admin; then "Processando" (videos at work, with their percent) and "Prontos" (the rest,
  newest first), both narrowed by the search on top. The person's photo, name and a menu (Conta, photo,
  sign out) sit at its foot. The top bar can hide the sidebar. Phones drop the sidebar and the tabs for
  a tab bar with Vídeos, Cortes, Painel and Conta.
- Tabs: every video opened becomes a tab in the top bar (mascot, title, close), remembered per browser,
  up to 8. Closing the tab in view moves to its neighbour or back to Vídeos. The plus after the tabs
  starts a new video.
- Video page: the page column holds the tabs Cortes · Original · Studio. Cortes: the picked clip plays
  at 9:16 beside the ranked list (the column's own width decides, through a container query); the open
  row shows Brilho's reason and the actions. Original: the full video with the clips marked on its bar
  and the transcript under it. From lg the inspector (400px) sits beside it: the video's mascot and
  state, the name in a field (opens rename), then Cortes, Origem, Arquivos and Ações as grouped cards.
  The top bar's last button hides the inspector anywhere.
- Studio (`?tab=studio`): a full-window editor with no sidebar, top bar, page header or tab bar. One
  bar: back to Cortes, the clip's title (the page's h1) with previous/next and "Corte N de M" to
  pick another, the draft state, undo/redo and "Renderizar corte". From 56rem of its own width: the
  Legendas · Estilo tabs on the left (400px, 440px from 72rem), the preview on the dark stage, and
  the timeline under both (trim handles, caption blocks; clicking a block opens it in Legendas).
  Narrower: the preview on top, then the tabs Trecho · Legendas · Estilo.

## Interaction rules (from CLAUDE.md)

- Clickable elements show the pointer cursor (global rule in `src/styles.css`).
- Disabled: `cursor-not-allowed`; loading: `cursor-progress` (`aria-busy`).
- Hover, press (`motion-safe:active:scale-[0.99]`) and focus (`focus-visible:ring-4`) on every
  interactive element.

## Layout and spacing

Based on the 8-point grid (Material, IBM Carbon), WCAG 2.5.8 target size and line-length research
(45–75 characters per line).

- **Spacing scale:** multiples of 4px, mostly 8px: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48. Space inside a group is
  smaller than the space between groups (inner ≤ outer).
- **Page rhythm:** sections of a page are 32px apart (`gap-8`); a section's title and its content 16px
  (`gap-4`); items in a list or grid 12–20px; parts of one card 8–12px. Card padding 16px on phones, 20–24px
  from `sm` up.
- **Shell:** top bar 56px; sidebar 288px on the frame; the panel fills the rest with 8px of frame on
  its trailing and bottom sides, 14px corners. Plain pages pad 32px inside the panel (16px on phones);
  the video page pads its column 32px and its inspector 24px.
- **Reading width:** paragraphs (page descriptions, clip reasons, empty states) stop at about 65
  characters (`max-w-prose`).
- **Control heights:** 40px for buttons, inputs, selects, chips and search in toolbars and cards; 44px for
  fields inside forms and dialogs; 32px only for small secondary buttons inside a dense row. Icon-only
  buttons are 40px (never under 32px, so the 24px WCAG minimum always holds with room to spare).
  Controls side by side always share one height.
- **Same piece, same size:** the search field is 288px wide from `sm` up and full width on phones.
- **Phones:** nothing sits alone on a row that it does not need; actions go full width under the
  content they act on.
- **Edge lines** (gridline pass, 2026-11-21, redrawn 2026-11-23 after the creators redesign; set by
  Claude at the person's request): measured on ink, not boxes, within 1px.
    - Sidebar (288px, enlarged 2026-11-23 at the owner's request): 44px rows with 15px labels and
      22px icons. Boxes 10px in (rows, storage card, account), icon column centred
      at 33, labels at 56, trailing line 22px in (counts, the upload glyph, the account menu glyph,
      the storage card's numbers). The two cards pad 11px to take back their 1px border, so their
      content starts on the icons' leading edge (22). The brand header pairs the 40px ghost tile
      with the uppercase NELCOTA wordmark. The 30px photo centres on the icon column.
    - Panel: the breadcrumb and every plain page's content share one text line, 32px in (16px on
      phones). The page bar's trailing icon buttons sit 24px in, so their glyph ends on the page's
      trailing line. The page bar and the sidebar's first row share one centre line.
    - Inspector: cards 24px in; rows 16px in on both sides with a 20px icon and the label 12px after
      it (56px rows); field labels and group titles 4px in from the cards, as in the reference.
    - Clip list: rows 16px in on both sides; the number badge is the icon column, the open row's
      details start on the title line after it (56px).
    - Tabs (video page, admin) have no side padding: the first label's icon sits on the text line.
    - Dialogs: the close glyph ends on `--dialog-padding` and centres on the title.
- **Dialogs are stepped** (2026-11-23, the owner's pick "A, Trilha" from `design/explore/modals/`):
  every dialog uses `StepDialogFrame`. A flow with more than one question gets the `StepRail`
  sidebar: the dialog's name, numbered steps joined by a line (done in green, current in blue,
  each with a one-line hint, the ones reached can be reopened) and "Passo X de N" with a bar.
  Phones hide the rail and show segments above the title. Each step asks one thing, starts filled
  in, and has Voltar on the leading side of the footer with the step's action trailing. A
  permanent action lists what goes away (`LossList`) before the step that does it. A single
  question (rename, Studio warnings, admin confirmations) uses the same frame without the rail.
